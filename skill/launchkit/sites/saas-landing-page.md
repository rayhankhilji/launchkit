# SaaS Landing Page

**No public submission route.** SaaS Landing Page (SLP) is a gallery of SaaS landing, pricing, features, about, FAQ and contact pages, with a templates section and deals. As of 2026-10-12 there is no submit link in the header, footer or sitemap, and `/submit/` is a 404. The only contacts are the "Advertise" page (https://slpads.framer.website/) and the advertising email in the footer. Sites are picked by the curator.

## Before you start
- Only worth a pitch for a polished SaaS marketing site.
- Advertising is paid. With budget 0, don't pursue it.

## Steps
1. Search https://saaslandingpage.com/ for `product.name`. If listed, mark `live` with the URL and stop.
2. Otherwise `launchkit mark saas-landing-page skipped --note "no public submission form, editorial only"`.
3. If the user wants to pitch the curator, draft a 3-line note (what it is, `product.url`, which page is strongest: landing, pricing or features) with `lk draft saas-landing-page -` and mark `needs_human`. Don't use the advertising email for a free listing request without the user's approval.

## Make the listing perform
- SLP organises by page type (Landing, Pricing, Features, About Us, Blog, Testimonials, FAQ, Contact Us) and by stack (React, Next.js, Tailwind, Webflow, Framer, GSAP) and tag (B2B, Productivity, Dark, FinTech). A distinctive pricing or features page is the most likely way in.

## Gotchas
- `/faq/` and `/contact-us/` are galleries of other companies' FAQ and contact pages, not SLP's own help pages.
- The sister site SaaS Interface (app UI inspiration) is linked in the banner; it also has no public form seen.

## After submitting
- Nothing to track unless the user pitched it.
