# MakeUseOf

Consumer tech how-to site (Valnet group) covering apps, productivity, Windows, Mac, Android, smart home and networking. It publishes many "best apps for X" lists and how-to guides, which suit useful consumer and prosumer tools.

## Before you start
- Fit: consumer or prosumer tools with a free tier, browser extensions, desktop and mobile apps. Pure enterprise B2B rarely fits.
- No tip form. The contact page lists department emails (seen):
  - editorial@makeuseof.com: topic ideas, feedback, corrections or suggestions. Use for product pitches.
  - pr@makeuseof.com: events and media inquiries.
  - partnerships@makeuseof.com and advertising@valnetinc.com: paid. Skip.
- Have screenshots, `links.demo_video`, and a promo code if there is a paid tier.

## Field map

| Email part | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| To | editorial@makeuseof.com | |
| Subject | under 80 chars | the use case, e.g. "Free tool to batch-rename photos by date on Windows and Mac" |
| Body | under 150 words | `copy.press_pitch` adapted: the reader problem, what the app does, platforms from `product.platforms`, price from `product.pricing_details`, `product.url`, promo code, `founder.name`, `founder.email` |

## Steps
1. Open https://www.makeuseof.com/contact/ and confirm the editorial address.
2. Draft the email with `launchkit draft makeuseof -` and mark `drafted`.
3. Send only with explicit approval. Mark `submitted` after the user confirms.

## Make the listing perform
- Pitch it as an answer to a search query people type ("how to ...", "best free ... app"). Their articles are written for search.
- Name the free alternative angle if it replaces a paid tool readers know.
- Also find a MakeUseOf writer who wrote a "best X apps" list in your category and pitch them directly with one line referencing that list. Lists get updated, and a new entry is a small ask.

## Gotchas
- Same Valnet structure as Android Police and How-To Geek. Don't send the same pitch to several Valnet sites on the same day.
- No auto-reply.

## After submitting
- Search makeuseof.com for `product.name` after 1-2 weeks.
