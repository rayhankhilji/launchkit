# Ben's Bites

Ben Tossell's AI newsletter on Substack (170k+ subscribers, sent Tuesdays and Thursdays) about building with AI agents plus tools worth trying. The old free community submission site news.bensbites.com now just redirects to bensbites.com, so the free route is gone. Getting in now means a paid placement or being picked by Ben himself.

## Before you start
- Only relevant for AI products or tools AI builders use.
- Paid placements (from the sponsor page, checked 2026-10-12): Primary $3,500, Secondary $2,800, Top Tools $2,000 per send, with 5-20% multi-send discounts. At budget 0, mark `skipped`.
- Prepare the copy in advance: 140 chars + 1 link for Top Tools, 300 chars + 2 links for Primary/Secondary.

## Field map

The sponsor page (a Notion page at sponsor.bensbites.com) has "Book main ad", "Book secondary ad" and "Book tool placement ad" buttons. The booking form behind them was not opened (unconfirmed fields).

| Slot | Limit | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Top Tools ("Tools and demos" section) | 140 chars + 1 link | `product.tagline` or `tagline_short` + `product.url` |
| Secondary ("What I'm consuming") | 300 chars + 2 links | must be a useful post/article/resource, not an ad: link a tutorial from `links.docs` or a blog post |
| Primary (end of intro) | 300 chars + 2 links | `product.description_short` + `product.url` + `links.demo_video` |
| Contact email | email | `founder.email` |

## Steps
1. Open https://sponsor.bensbites.com/ and check current prices and dates.
2. If no budget: mark `skipped` with "paid only; news.bensbites.com closed".
3. If budget allows: draft the copy for the chosen slot with `launchkit draft ben-s-bites -`, mark `needs_human` with "choose slot and pay on sponsor page". The user books and pays.

## Make the listing perform
- Top Tools reaches people in evaluation mode and is the cheapest slot; it fits a tool with a free tier or trial they can try today.
- Readers build with Claude, MCP, agents and no-code tools. Say which of those your product plugs into.
- For organic mentions, Ben shares tools he actually uses. A short demo video on X tagging @bentossell is the realistic free path, and only if the user wants to post it.

## Gotchas
- Don't send the user to news.bensbites.com; it's gone.
- The Secondary slot rejects plain product ads. It needs content.

## After submitting
- If a placement is booked, record the send date in the note and check the issue on that day.
