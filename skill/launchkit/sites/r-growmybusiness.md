# r/growmybusiness

155k small business owners and founders asking growth questions. You can't sell here, but you can ask for feedback on your product, which is the useful angle for a launch. Traffic per post is small (single or low double digit upvotes), so keep the effort low.

## Before you start
- Text posts only (no images, video or link posts).
- The title must either contain a question mark or the word "feedback". This is enforced by the submit rules.
- No blog posts, no external surveys or feedback forms (Rules 4 and 5).

## Field map

| Field on the site | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Post type tab | Text only | Text |
| Title | 300 chars, must include "?" or "feedback" | a specific ask, e.g. "Feedback on my onboarding for <product.tagline_short>" or "How do you get the first 10 users for a <category> tool?" |
| Body | Markdown | 2-3 short paragraphs: who it's for (`product.target_audience`), what it does (`product.description_short`), what you've tried, the exact thing you want feedback on, `product.url` at the end |
| Flair | Question, Feedback (Monthly Tips is mod-only) | Feedback when the post is about the product, Question for a growth question |

Advice content (a strategy write-up) goes in the stickied **Monthly Growth Strategy & Advice Thread** as a comment. Links and a signature are allowed there as long as the comment has real advice.

## Steps
1. Open https://www.reddit.com/r/growmybusiness/ and skim the newest Feedback posts for tone.
2. Draft a Feedback post. The ask must be real and narrow (pricing page, onboarding, positioning). Optionally draft an advice comment for the monthly thread from `copy.launch_post_story`.
3. `launchkit draft r-growmybusiness -` with title, body, flair. `launchkit mark r-growmybusiness drafted`.
4. Only with posting allowed and user approval: click **Create Post**, choose Text, fill title and body, pick flair, Post. Screenshot to `.launchkit/evidence/r-growmybusiness.png`, `launchkit mark r-growmybusiness submitted --listing-url <url>`.

## Make the listing perform
- Posts that got comments were honest about where the founder is stuck ("first two customers came fast, then 3.5 weeks of nothing"). Lead with that, then the product.
- Ask one question people can answer from a 30-second look. Broad "thoughts?" gets ignored.
- Reply to each comment and say what you will change.

## Gotchas
- "Try it free and give feedback" offers are fine. Discount codes, sales pitches and upsells are not (Rule 3).
- A link to a Google Form or Typeform gets the post removed.

## After submitting
- Check the post shows in /new while logged out. Follow up in the comments within a day.
