# Lenny's Newsletter (jobs/community)

Lenny Rachitsky's product and growth newsletter (1M+ subscribers). Three surfaces sound relevant, but none takes open submissions: Product Pass (a free year of 30+ partner tools for annual subscribers), Lenny's Jobs (a job board powered by TrueUp) and a paid-subscriber Slack community. Partners in Product Pass are established companies (Lovable, Replit, Supabase, Granola). Treat this as a long shot for early products.

## Before you start
- No form, pricing page or partner application was found for Product Pass. Partnerships seem to be by invitation or direct deal (unconfirmed).
- Product Pass partners give a year free of a paid plan to every annual subscriber. The user must be willing to give away that much.
- Lenny's Jobs lists jobs only (via trueup.io). Relevant only if the company is hiring.

## Field map

| Route | Contact | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Product Pass partner | no public address found | skip unless the user has a warm intro to Lenny's team |
| Community meetup sponsorship (paid) | meetup@lennyrachitsky.com | `company.legal_name`, `founder.email`, budget |
| Slack community | paid subscribers only | not a listing; don't self-promote there |
| Lenny's Jobs | lennysjobs.com (TrueUp) | only if hiring; jobs come from TrueUp's index |

## Steps
1. Open https://www.lennysproductpass.com/ and check whether a "partner with us" link has appeared (none on 2026-10-12).
2. If none and no warm intro: `launchkit mark lenny-s-newsletter-jobs-community skipped --note "invite-only partners; no public form"`.
3. If the user wants meetup sponsorship and has budget: draft a short email to meetup@lennyrachitsky.com, mark `drafted` and wait for approval.

## Make the listing perform
- Product Pass partners are tools PMs and founders use daily (AI builders, notes, analytics, design). A product only has a chance if it's already known in that crowd.
- A better free path: get the founder interviewed or quoted in a Lenny post. That's earned media, not something launchkit can submit.

## Gotchas
- Don't post promos in the Friends of Lenny Slack; it's for paid members and self-promo is frowned on.
- Codes run out and offers expire, so partners commit to a fixed number of codes.

## After submitting
- Nothing to track unless a sponsorship email was sent.
