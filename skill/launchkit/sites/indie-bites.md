# Indie Bites

James McKinven's podcast of 15-minute interviews with bootstrapped founders, plus a weekly newsletter, The Indie Bite. Note: the latest episode on the site was E130 on 30 Oct 2025, almost a year before this check, so the show looks paused. Pitch only if the founder has a real bootstrapped story with revenue.

## Before you start
- Fit check: guests are founders of small, profitable, bootstrapped businesses who share numbers (MRR) and how they got first customers. Pre-revenue products don't fit.
- No guest form. The about page says "Any questions. DM me on Twitter" (@jmckinven).
- Needs `founder.bio_short`, `company.funding_stage` (should be bootstrapped) and a revenue figure the founder is happy to share.

## Field map

| Pitch part | Limit | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Channel | X DM | to @jmckinven from the founder's X account (`founder.twitter`) |
| Who | 1 sentence | `founder.name`, `founder.role`, `product.name` |
| Story hook | 1-2 sentences | how it started and where it is now, only facts from the profile and what the founder confirms |
| Topic offer | 1 line | one lesson for indie hackers, e.g. first 100 customers, pricing change |
| Link | URL | `product.url` |

## Steps
1. Open https://indiebites.com/episodes and check whether new episodes have appeared since E130.
2. If still inactive, mark `skipped` with "podcast inactive since Oct 2025".
3. If active, write a 60-80 word DM per the map, `launchkit draft indie-bites -`, mark `drafted`. Only the founder sends it, from their own X account.

## Make the listing perform
- Lead with a number and a lesson ("$4k MRR in 8 months from one SEO page"). The show sells on concrete takeaways.
- Episodes are short, so offer one sharp topic, not a life story.

## Gotchas
- The $60/year "Indie Feast" membership is for listeners, not a listing.
- The newsletter takes no submissions; it summarises the podcast and indie news.

## After submitting
- If James replies, hand scheduling to the founder. Mark `needs_human` with "podcast interview to schedule".
