# Godly

**Godly is gone as of 2026-10-12.** godly.website now 301-redirects to recent.design. Recent's info page says it "takes the place of Godly" with a broader curated feed (websites, launch videos, OG images, app icons, tools). There is no public submission form on Recent; the team picks work itself. The only paid option is sponsorship ($5,000 for 30 days), which is advertising, not a listing.

## Before you start
- Nothing to fill. Don't buy a sponsorship.

## Steps
1. Search recent.design/websites for `product.name`. If it's there, `launchkit mark godly live --listing-url <url>`.
2. Otherwise `launchkit mark godly skipped --note "Godly closed; replaced by recent.design, no submissions"`.

## Make the listing perform
- Recent started by collecting design work posted on X and now also pulls from Instagram. A short screen recording of the site posted on X by the founder (`links.twitter`) is the realistic way to get noticed.

## Gotchas
- Old lists still say "submit to Godly". The form no longer exists.
