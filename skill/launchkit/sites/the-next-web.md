# The Next Web

Amsterdam-based tech news site and conference (TNW is now "A Tekpon Company"). Sections cover deep tech, sustainability, ecosystems, fintech, future of work, startups and funding, with a European slant. There is no public tips address or editorial form: https://thenextweb.com/contact returns a 404, the only site-wide mailto is conference@thenextweb.com, and "Get in touch" goes to the paid partnerships page. So the only free route is pitching an individual reporter.

## Before you start
- A European or deep-tech angle helps; plain launches rarely get written up.
- Find a reporter: open a recent article in the matching section (e.g. "Startups and technology", "Investors and funding") and note the byline. Reporter emails are not listed on the site (unconfirmed); use their public profile or LinkedIn only if the user is comfortable.
- No form, no account.

## Field map

| Field on the site | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| To | email or DM | the reporter found above |
| Subject | under 80 chars | the headline you'd want |

| Sign-off | text | `founder.name`, `founder.role`, `founder.email` |

## Steps
1. Open https://thenextweb.com/ and pick the section matching `product.categories[0]`.
2. Pick one reporter who covered something similar recently.
3. `lk draft the-next-web -` with recipient, subject and body; mark `drafted`. If no reporter contact can be found without an account, mark `needs_human` with "pick a TNW reporter to pitch".
4. Send only with user approval.

## Make the listing perform
- TNW likes European startups with a sharp story (policy, sustainability, AI with a real-world test). Tie the pitch to a current theme on their homepage.
- The TNW Conference (Amsterdam) has startup programmes; speaking or exhibiting is often how startups get noticed there. Mention it to the user as an option, not as part of this pitch.

## Gotchas
- "TELL YOUR STORY" on https://thenextweb.com/partnerships and TNW Council membership are paid and fee-based. Skip with budget 0.
- Don't email conference@thenextweb.com with a news pitch; it's for events.
