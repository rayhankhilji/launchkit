# AI Breakfast

A beehiiv newsletter sent about three times a week with AI news and product analysis, read by 100,000+ subscribers. The editorial is news-led (model launches, big lab moves, funding). There is no tool submission form, so the realistic route for a small product is a paid sponsorship.

## Before you start
- Only relevant if the product is AI-related.
- Sponsorship costs money: confirm `launch_settings.budget_usd` covers it before writing anything. At budget 0, mark `skipped`.
- Prepare a 2-3 sentence product blurb and one link.

## Field map

No web form. Two contact routes are given at the bottom of every issue: reply to the issue email, or DM @aibreakfast on X.

| What they need | Format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Who you are | 1 line | `founder.name`, `founder.role`, `product.name` |
| What it is | 2-3 sentences | `product.description_short` plus one concrete `product.key_features[0]` |
| Link | URL | `product.url` |
| Ask | 1 line | "What are your current sponsor rates and open dates?" |
| Reply-to | email | `founder.email` |

## Steps
1. Open https://aibreakfast.beehiiv.com/ and read the latest issue to see the "In partnership with" sponsor format.
2. If the budget allows a sponsorship, write a short sponsor enquiry (under 100 words) from the map above.
3. Save it with `launchkit draft ai-breakfast -` noting the route: reply to an issue email (needs the user subscribed) or X DM to @aibreakfast.
4. Mark `drafted`. Never send from the user's email or X account without explicit approval. If approved and sent, mark `needs_human` with "awaiting sponsor rates".

## Make the listing perform
- Sponsored slots sit at the top under "In partnership with". Lead with the one thing a reader can try in a minute.
- AI Breakfast readers follow frontier model news, so tie the product to a model or capability they already know about.
- If you only want free coverage: real news (a notable launch, open-source release or funding) is the only plausible hook, and there's no tips address, so don't spend time on it.

## Gotchas
- The site's Upgrade page is a reader premium membership (ad-free), not a listing option.
- Subscribing is free but adds an email the user may not want; ask first.

## After submitting
- Wait for a rate card reply, then hand the decision back to the user.
