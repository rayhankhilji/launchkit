# AI Library

Phygital+'s free AI tools library (3,600+ tools), aimed at designers and creators. The submit form is short and asks for no login. It even accepts Colab notebooks and other free tools.

## Before you start
- Nothing to buy or sign up for.
- Have `product.url`, `product.description_short`, and a public handle (`founder.twitter` or `links.twitter`).

## Field map

| Field on the site | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Link to the tool | text | `product.url` |
| Describe the tool | textarea | `product.description_medium`: what you can do with it and what it's for |
| (unlabeled textarea) | textarea | leave empty |
| Social handle or contact | textarea | `founder.twitter`, else `links.twitter`, else `submission.email`. This is shown publicly as the submitter |
| (unlabeled textarea) | textarea | leave empty |

## Steps
1. Open https://library.phygital.plus/tool-submission.
2. Fill the link and description. Put the public handle in the contact box. Use the email only if the user is OK with it being public.
3. Click **Send**. Screenshot to `.launchkit/evidence/ai-library.png`.
4. `launchkit mark ai-library submitted`

## Make the listing perform
- The audience is visual creators (3D, image, video, design). Describe the creative use case first, in their words ("generate textures", "edit product photos").
- Mention if there's a free tier or free trial. The library leans toward tools people can try right away.

## Gotchas
- The contact field is public. Don't put a personal email there without asking.
- The unlabeled boxes have no clear purpose. Leave them empty rather than guessing.

## After submitting
- No confirmation email was promised. Search the library for `product.name` after a few weeks.
