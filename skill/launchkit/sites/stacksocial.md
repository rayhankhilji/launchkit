# StackSocial

StackSocial is the consumer deals shop run by StackCommerce. Vendors do not self-list. You send an inquiry to StackCommerce, whose team decides on placement on StackSocial, its white-label partner shops (1,000+ publishers) and paid placements. The old vendor page (stackcommerce.com/vendors) returns 404.

## Before you start
- Terms are negotiated, and premium placements are paid. Mark `needs_human` ("requires deal terms decision").
- Fit: StackSocial is a consumer-facing deals shop. Check with the user that a consumer discount makes sense for the product before sending.
- Background on what they offer vendors: https://www.stackcommerce.com/advertise (curated marketplace placement, partner shops, Impact affiliate network, email, SMS, web push and newsletter promotion).

## Field map
StackCommerce contact form, in order:

| Field on the site | Type | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Company Name | required | `company.legal_name` or `product.name` |
| Website | required | `product.url` |
| Monthly Unique Visitors | optional | leave blank unless the user gives a number |
| Social Followers | optional | leave blank unless known |
| Do you have an email newsletter? | Yes / No | ask user, default No |
| Interested In | Monetizing my audience, Promoting my brand, Selling my product, Getting in touch, I'm not sure | **Selling my product** |
| Name | required | `founder.name` |
| Email | required | `founder.email` |
| Phone Number | optional | leave blank unless the user gives one |
| How did you hear about Stack? | dropdown | Search Engine |
| Sign Up for Our Newsletter | checkbox | leave unchecked |

## Steps
1. Open https://www.stackcommerce.com/contact.
2. Fill the fields from the map. Choose **Selling my product**.
3. Click **Submit**. If the invisible reCAPTCHA escalates to a challenge, hand off to the user.
4. Screenshot to `.launchkit/evidence/stacksocial.png`; `launchkit mark stacksocial submitted --note "Vendor inquiry sent to StackCommerce"`.

## Make the listing perform
- StackSocial deals are discount-led (bundles, lifetime plans, big percentage off). Have a consumer-friendly offer ready for the sales call.
- Their reach comes from partner publishers, so a clear product name, a short benefit line and strong screenshots matter more than technical detail.

## Gotchas
- There is no free self-serve listing. Do not report this as "listed" until StackCommerce confirms a deal.
- No vendor form was found on stacksocial.com itself; use the StackCommerce contact form.

## After submitting
- A partnerships rep replies by email if interested. Pass terms to the user.
