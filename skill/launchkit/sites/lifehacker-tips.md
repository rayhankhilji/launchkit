# Lifehacker (tips)

Ziff Davis site with practical tech and life how-tos, app recommendations and deals. The site is live, but there is no public tips page or tips email: /contact-us, /about-us and /staff return 404, and the Ziff Davis contacts page lists only corporate addresses. Pitch individual writers.

## Before you start
- Fit: consumer apps that solve an everyday problem (productivity, money, home, health, browser extensions). B2B tools rarely fit.
- Find 2-3 writers who recently covered the same kind of app: search lifehacker.com for `product.categories[0]` or a competitor in `product.competitors_alternatives`, open the article, and use the contact or social links on the author bio.
- Have a promo code for paid tiers and `links.demo_video` or `assets.demo_gif`. Writers test before they write.

## Field map
No form. Email or social DM to a writer:

| Part | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | under 70 chars | the tip itself, e.g. "Free app that blocks shorts on YouTube" |
| Body | under 120 words | one sentence on the reader problem, one on what the app does, price from `product.pricing_details`, platforms from `product.platforms`, `product.url`, promo code, `founder.name` |

## Steps
1. Search the site and pick writers by beat. Note each writer's public contact method.
2. Write one short pitch per writer, each mentioning the article of theirs that made you choose them. Save all in `launchkit draft lifehacker-tips -` and mark `drafted`.
3. Don't send emails or DMs without explicit approval. Mark `submitted` once the user confirms.

## Make the listing perform
- Pitch the hack, not the company. "How to get X done in 30 seconds" is a Lifehacker headline; "Acme raises seed round" is not.
- Free apps or generous free tiers get covered more. Say clearly what is free.
- Timely angles help: back-to-school, tax season, new year planning, a fresh OS update that breaks an old workflow.
- If the app replaces a paid tool readers know, name it.

## Gotchas
- Deals content is affiliate-driven. A deal pitch is a commercial request and may be ignored.
- No central inbox means mass emails to every writer look like spam. Keep it to 2-3 writers.

## After submitting
- Search lifehacker.com for `product.name` after 1-2 weeks. If covered, share the article and add it to `links.press_kit`.
