# SaaSFrame

**No public submission route.** SaaSFrame is a paid UX/UI library ($15-18/month for members) of SaaS website pages, product screens, flows and emails, with Figma copies. The team picks the companies. As of 2026-10-12 the site, sitemap and about page have no submit or suggest form; `/submit` is a 404. The only forms are the Studio quote form (a Figma-to-Webflow build service) and newsletter signup.

## Before you start
- Nothing to fill. Being listed depends on the product being an established SaaS with interesting flows.

## Steps
1. Search https://www.saasframe.io/saas for `product.name`. If the company is in the library, mark `live` with its `/saas/<name>` URL and stop.
2. Otherwise `launchkit mark saasframe skipped --note "no public submission form, editorial only"`.
3. Don't use the Studio form; it's a paid build-service quote request, not a listing form.

## Make the listing perform
- SaaSFrame indexes by page category (Landing, Pricing, Changelog, Careers...), section (Hero, Bento Grid, Pricing Plan Comparison...) and product flows (Sign Up, Onboarding, Paywall). Products with clear onboarding and pricing flows are the ones it documents.

## Gotchas
- Pricing on the site is for buying access to the library, not for getting listed.
- Forms on the site use Cloudflare Turnstile.
