# G2

The biggest B2B software review site, and since early 2026 the owner of Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice. A free profile is enough to start collecting reviews. Worth doing for any B2B product.

Checked 2026-10-12: the process page https://sell.g2.com/create-a-profile is live and describes the flow below. The form at /products/new itself served a bot challenge, so its exact fields are unconfirmed.

## Before you start
- Business email on the product's domain (`submission.email`). Login options: LinkedIn, Facebook, Google or business email.
- `assets.logo_square`, `product.description_medium`, a category idea from `product.categories[0]`.
- B2B only. Consumer apps: mark `skipped` with "not B2B".

## Field map (unconfirmed; from G2's public process page and FAQ)

| Field on the site | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Product or service name | text | `product.name` |
| Product website | URL | `product.url` |
| Vendor / company name | text | `company.legal_name` or `product.name` |
| Category suggestion | text/select | closest G2 category to `product.categories[0]` (check a competitor's G2 page for theirs) |
| Description | ~500-1000 chars | `product.description_medium` |
| Your name / job title | text | `founder.name` / `founder.role` |
| Business email | email | `submission.email` |
| Logo (later, in my.g2 -> Product Profile -> Product Information) | image | `assets.logo_square` |

## Steps
1. Search g2.com for `product.name`. If a profile exists and says **Unclaimed**, click that box and follow the claim prompts (go to step 4).
2. Open https://www.g2.com/products/new. If a challenge page or CAPTCHA appears, mark `needs_human` with "G2 bot check, open g2.com/products/new by hand".
3. Fill the request form from the map and submit. G2 calls this "conditionally approved"; their research team verifies it in about 3-5 business days and places it in a category.
4. Once the profile is live, click **Claim** on it. Claim review takes 1-3 business days.
5. Screenshot each confirmation to `.launchkit/evidence/g2.png`.
6. `launchkit mark g2 submitted --note "request sent, 3-5 days vetting, then claim"`

## Make the listing perform
- G2 says claimed profiles get about 44% more traffic than unclaimed ones. Always finish the claim step.
- Reviews decide category rank and the seasonal Grid reports. After claim, give the user the review link from my.g2 to send to real customers. Never write or incentivise fake reviews.
- Pick the category your competitors sit in (`product.competitors_alternatives`), so you appear on their comparison pages.
- Fill features, pricing and screenshots in my.g2 right after claim; empty profiles look abandoned.

## Gotchas
- Paid plans (Buyer Intent, content) are sales-led. Skip them with budget 0.
- A G2 rep usually reaches out after approval. If they ask for a call, mark `needs_human`.

## After submitting
- Check the profile URL `https://www.g2.com/products/<slug>/reviews`. Review activity is under Review Management in my.g2.
- Then run `capterra` and `getapp`, which now come from the same listing.
