# r/productivity

4.3M members, and **no promotion of any kind is allowed**. A pinned mod post says advertising means an instant ban, even if disclosed, even just mentioning that you have an app, and even having the product name in your username. There are no designated promo threads (as of 2026-10-12), so the old "promo in designated threads" note no longer applies. Skip it.

## Before you start
- Text posts only. AI-written posts and comments are banned outright.
- The mods suggest paid Reddit ads targeting r/productivity instead. That is a budget decision for the user, not something to do here.
