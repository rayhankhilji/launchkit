# r/indiehackers

196k bootstrapped founders. Self-promotion is allowed **once per product**, with the **Self Promotion** flair, and the post must ask for feedback or critique rather than advertise. Good fit for any indie product. (This is the subreddit, not indiehackers.com.)

## Before you start
- One shot per product, so make it count. Check the user hasn't already posted this product here.
- Brand-new Reddit accounts are told to contact the mods via modmail first.
- Any MRR, ARR, revenue or user numbers need proof (a Stripe shareable live-data link, or a screenshot in edge cases). Leave numbers out unless the profile has them and the user can prove them.

## Field map

| Field on the site | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Post type tab | Text / Images & Video / Link | Text (add one screenshot inline if helpful) |
| Title | 300 chars | the specific ask or angle, e.g. "Looking for 5 <target_audience> to test <what it does>" or "I removed X before launch, here's why" |
| Body | Markdown | from `copy.launch_post_story`: why you built it, what it does, what you want feedback on, pricing from `product.pricing_details`, `product.url` at the end |
| Flair | required: Self Promotion, Sharing story/journey/experience, General Question, Knowledge post | **Self Promotion** for any post about the user's product |

## Steps
1. Open https://www.reddit.com/r/indiehackers/ and read the two pinned rule posts ("NEW RULES..." and "Community Reorganisation & New Posting Guidelines").
2. Draft a Self Promotion post built around one concrete question for feedback.
3. `launchkit draft r-indiehackers -` with title, body and flair. `launchkit mark r-indiehackers drafted`.
4. Only with posting allowed and user approval: Create Post, Text tab, fill title and body, set flair **Self Promotion**, Post. Screenshot to `.launchkit/evidence/r-indiehackers.png`, `launchkit mark r-indiehackers submitted --listing-url <url>`.

## Make the listing perform
- The best Self Promotion posts during research had a hook beyond "I built X": a decision ("I removed all monetization right before launch"), a niche ("Outbid-style site for independent musicians"), or a tight ask ("Looking for 5 founders who sell to SaaS companies").
- End with a question the reader can answer. Those posts got 40-200 comments.
- Post on a weekday, early US time, and answer every comment for the first few hours.

## Gotchas
- Using "Sharing story" flair for what is really a product pitch gets a warning and then a ban.
- No fake Q&A (asking from one account, answering from another), no upvote rings, no "what are you building today?" karma threads.
- The mods have said they may restrict self-promotion to certain weekdays. Recheck the pinned posts before posting.

## After submitting
- Stay in the thread. Turn useful feedback into a follow-up "Sharing story" post weeks later, about what you learned, not the product.
