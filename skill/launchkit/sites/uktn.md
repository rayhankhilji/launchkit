# UKTN

UK tech trade publication (formerly Tech City News, since 2012) for founders, investors and professional services. Covers early-stage startups through unicorns across all UK regions, with a strong focus on funding news, policy and startup profiles. Pitches go to the editor by email.

## Before you start
- Best fit: `company.country` = UK. Note `company.city`: UKTN has regional hub pages (London, North West, Scotland, Wales...), and regional angles help.
- News hook: funding round, regional first, notable hire, policy angle.
- Email only; no form.

## Field map

| Field on the site | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| To | email | `editor@uktech.news` ("For pitches or editorial enquiries" on the contact page) |
| Subject | under 80 chars | headline, e.g. "`company.city` startup `product.name` raises `company.funding_raised`" |
| Body | under 150 words | `copy.press_pitch` with the UK region, round size, investors, what the money is for, plus `product.url` and `links.press_kit` |
| Sign-off | text | `founder.name`, `founder.role`, `founder.email` |

## Steps
1. Open https://www.uktech.news/contact and confirm the editor address.
2. Check the region's hub page for recent similar stories.
3. Write the email; offer an embargo if the news is not public. `lk draft uktn -`, mark `drafted`.
4. Send only with user approval.

## Make the listing perform
- UKTN "follows the money": funding rounds with named investors are the most reliable coverage.
- Mention the region in the first line. Stories outside London stand out.
- Founder opinion pieces run in the Opinion section; pitch one if there's no funding news.

## Gotchas
- partnerships@uktech.news and the media kit are for paid placements. Skip with budget 0.
- Don't send pitches to hi@uktech.news; that's general enquiries.

## After submitting
- No auto-reply. One follow-up after 3-5 working days.
