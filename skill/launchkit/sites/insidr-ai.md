# Insidr.ai

An AI education and community brand (AI tools list, courses, agency program, newsletter) with a curated "AI Tools Directory" of 500+ tools in 78+ categories. Submissions are suggestions: "If approved, we will add it to our AI Tools directory." No account and no fee on the submit form; paid exposure is sold separately under "Promotions".

## Before you start
- Text only: a message, the link and a tag. No uploads.
- Approval is at the editors' discretion and they only add a small share of tools. Low effort, so still worth doing.

## Field map

| Field on the site | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Message | textarea | 2-3 sentences from `product.description_medium`: what it does, who it's for, pricing from `product.pricing_details` |
| Link | URL | `product.url` |
| Tag | text | one category in their wording that matches `product.categories[0]` (e.g. "Productivity", "Marketing", "Copywriting", "Video Tools", "Customer Support") |

## Steps
1. Search the directory for `product.name`. If listed, mark `live` and stop.
2. Open https://www.insidr.ai/submit-tools/ (footer link "Submit AI Tools").
3. Fill Message, Link and Tag. The newsletter boxes on the same page are separate beehiiv embeds; leave them alone.
4. Submit, screenshot the confirmation to `.launchkit/evidence/insidr-ai.png`.
5. `launchkit mark insidr-ai submitted --note "editorial suggestion"`

## Make the listing perform
- Their directory is organised by business task (productivity, marketing, website builder, SEO, e-commerce, CRM and automation, customer support). Use one of those words in the Tag and the first sentence so the editor can place it quickly.
- The audience is non-technical business owners. Write the message in plain outcome terms, not developer jargon.

## Gotchas
- Don't use the "Submit AI News" form for a tool; it is for news items.

## After submitting
- No status updates. Search the directory in a few weeks and mark `live` if it appears.
