# Mindstream

A daily AI news newsletter on beehiiv (200k+ readers), now part of HubSpot (the signup and privacy notices are HubSpot's). It covers consumer-friendly AI news in short, jokey posts plus a weekly "10 Questions for AI Leaders" interview. No tool submission form, tips address or advertise page was found on the site.

## Before you start
- Fit: AI products with a consumer or business-user angle. Developer-only tools rarely fit their tone.
- No public pitch channel was found on 2026-10-12 (mindstream.news/advertise is a 404). Sponsorship likely goes through HubSpot's media sales (unconfirmed).
- At budget 0 there's no paid route, and the free route is earned news coverage only.

## Field map

| Route | Contact | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial pitch | no published address; authors listed at mindstream.news/authors | only for real news: launch with a story, funding, or data |
| "10 Questions for AI Leaders" | no form | an AI leader at an established company; usually not an early founder |
| Sponsorship | not published (HubSpot media, unconfirmed) | skip at budget 0 |

## Steps
1. Open https://www.mindstream.news/ and check the footer for any new advertise or contact link.
2. If none: `launchkit mark mindstream skipped --note "no public submission or sponsor channel"`.
3. If the user has real news and wants to try, draft a 3-sentence pitch from `copy.press_pitch` (`launchkit draft mindstream -`) and mark `needs_human` with "find an editor contact (LinkedIn: Mindstream)".

## Make the listing perform
- Their headlines are playful ("Someone confiscate the printer"). A pitch with a fun, concrete consumer angle has a better chance than a feature list.
- They cover what mainstream readers can try today. Make sure the product works without a waitlist.

## Gotchas
- Subscribing signs the user up for HubSpot marketing emails.

## After submitting
- Nothing to track unless a pitch was sent.
