# Silicon Canals

Note: this is no longer a startup news site. Silicon Canals was founded in Amsterdam in 2014 to cover Dutch and European startups, but Brown Brothers Media bought it in November 2025 and it now covers "Technology & Power", "Work & Digital Life" and "Relationships & Society" (behavioural research, institutions, long reads). Funding and launch news are not what it publishes any more. For most products, mark `skipped` with "outlet changed focus, no startup coverage".

## Before you start
- Only pitch if the product has a real angle for the new desks: data about how people work, research findings, or a story about power and incentives in tech. A product launch alone will not fit.
- Needs a primary source: the contact page asks you to identify "the primary source, filing, paper, announcement or person supporting the pitch".

## Field map

| Field on the site | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| To | email | `editorial@siliconcanals.com` |
| Subject | short | The finding or story, not the product name |
| Body | inline text, or a Word / accessible Google Doc | 3-5 sentences: the claim, the source behind it (link), why it matters for work or digital life, then `product.name` and `product.url` as context |
| Sign-off | text | `founder.name`, `founder.role`, `founder.email` |

## Steps
1. Open https://siliconcanals.com/contact/ and read the current desks on https://siliconcanals.com/about-us/.
2. Decide with the profile whether there is a non-promotional angle. If not, `lk mark silicon-canals skipped --note "no startup coverage since 2025 relaunch"`.
3. If there is, write the pitch, link the primary source, and `lk draft silicon-canals -`. Mark `drafted`.
4. Send only with user approval.

## Make the listing perform
- Pitch a finding, not a feature. "Our data from 4,000 freelancers shows invoices get paid 9 days later in August" works; "Acme launches invoicing" does not.
- Use only numbers that are in the profile or the user's own data, and say where they came from.

## Gotchas
- The site says it does not accept paid links, link exchanges or unrelated guest posts. Don't offer them.
- Old startup articles stay in the archive, which is why directory lists still show it as a startup outlet.

## After submitting
- No auto-reply. One follow-up after a week at most.
