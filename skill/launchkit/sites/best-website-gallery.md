# Best Website Gallery

**No public submission route as of 2026-10-12.** The site is live (2,600+ sites, a daily Site of the Day), but it is a personal collection curated by David Hellmann since 2008. There is no submit page (/submit returns 404), no form, and no submission address. The only listed email (david@hellmann.io, in the legal notice) is for removal or correction requests.

## Before you start
- Nothing to fill. Do not email the curator to pitch the site unless the user explicitly asks; the published address is for rights holders.

## Steps
1. Search https://bestwebsite.gallery/sites for `product.name`. If it's there, `launchkit mark best-website-gallery live --listing-url <url>`.
2. Otherwise `launchkit mark best-website-gallery skipped --note "no public submissions; curator picks sites himself"`.

## Make the listing perform
- The curator finds sites on other galleries and on social media. Getting into Awwwards, Godly-style feeds or Land-book is the indirect way in.
- Community ratings exist but do not affect inclusion or Site of the Day.

## Gotchas
- Older directory lists still show this as a free submission site. It isn't.

## After submitting
- Nothing to track.
