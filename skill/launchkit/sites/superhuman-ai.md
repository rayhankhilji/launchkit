# Superhuman AI

A daily AI newsletter by Zain Kahn for business professionals, about 1.5M+ readers (2M+ counting its sister newsletter The Code). The homepage at https://www.superhuman.ai/ is only a subscribe form. There is no free "submit a tool" route; tools appear through paid placements sold at https://advertise.superhuman.ai/.

## Before you start
- With `launch_settings.budget_usd` at 0: mark `skipped` ("paid only"). Don't fill the media kit form unless the user wants pricing.
- If the user has budget, they need to decide the placement and dates themselves on a sales call. Mark `needs_human`.
- Have ready for the call: `product.name`, `product.url`, `product.description_short`, `product.target_audience`, `assets.logo_square`, one screenshot.

## Field map

The advertise site has two entry points: a "Grab the media kit" form (30 seconds, exact fields not visible without JS, unconfirmed) and "Book a 15-minute call". Ad formats listed on the page:

| Placement | Limit | Where / when | Clicks per send (their numbers) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsored tutorial | written by their team, image + CTA | top, Mon and Thu, selected brands only | 1,500-3,000 |
| Primary | up to 550 chars, image + CTA | top, Mon-Sun | 1,000-4,000 |
| Secondary | up to 350 chars, image + CTA | middle, Mon-Sun | 750-2,000 |
| Tool listing | up to 150 chars | bottom tools section, Mon-Fri | 500-1,000 |

Fill the copy from `product.description_short` (150 chars) or `product.description_medium` trimmed to the limit. They say their editorial team drafts the ad from your bullet points.

## Steps
1. Open https://advertise.superhuman.ai/ and check the formats and FAQ (pricing is only in the media kit or on a call).
2. If budget is 0: `launchkit mark superhuman-ai skipped --note "paid placements only"`.
3. If the user has budget: draft 3-5 bullet points from `product.key_features` and the 150-char tool line, save with `launchkit draft superhuman-ai -`, and mark `needs_human` with "book call or request media kit".

## Make the listing perform
- The cheapest test is the bottom tool listing (150 chars). Write it as one outcome sentence for a non-technical professional.
- Readers are managers and execs using AI at work. Lead with the job it does ("Turns sales calls into CRM notes"), not the model or stack.
- Ask for a date near a launch so the click spike stacks with other channels.

## Gotchas
- Max 4 sponsors per issue, so slots can be booked weeks out.
- The www.superhuman.ai/tools path is a 404; there is no public tools directory to submit to.

## After submitting
- Nothing to check unless the user books a placement. Ask them for the issue date and log it.
