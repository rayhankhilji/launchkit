# AI Wiki

**No longer accepts tool listings.** aiwiki.ai is now a sourced encyclopedia of AI models, companies, people and policy (about 4,700 articles). Its "Tools" section is the site's own calculators, not a directory of third-party products. Skip.

## Before you start
- Nothing to prepare. There's no submission form for products.
- The live site loads in a browser; a plain HTTP fetch hits a Vercel security checkpoint (429), so use the browser tool if re-checking.

## Field map

No submission form. The only contribution paths are "Suggest a correction, add a source, or research a missing topic" on articles, which are for encyclopedia content, not listings.

## Steps
1. Search the wiki (Cmd+K) for `product.name`. A page will only exist for notable products or companies with independent sources.
2. `launchkit mark ai-wiki skipped --note "encyclopedia, no product submissions"`

## Make the listing perform
- Not applicable. Writing a self-promotional article would break their verifiability policy and get reverted.
- If the company later gets independent press coverage, a reader-submitted "missing topic" suggestion is the honest route, and that's the user's call, not an automated submission.

## Gotchas
- Older directory lists describe this as an AI tools wiki. That's out of date.

## After submitting
- Nothing to do.
