# Hacker Newsletter

A weekly email (Fridays, 60,000+ subscribers, run by Kale Davis since 2010) that hand-picks the best Hacker News
links of the week. There is no submission form and no sponsor page on the site. The only way in is to do well on HN.

## Before you start
- Nothing to fill in here. This entry depends on the `hacker-news-show-hn` guide and `copy.show_hn_title` /
  `copy.show_hn_body`.

## Field map

| Field on the site | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| (none) | - | Only a subscribe box. No submission or contact form |

## Steps
1. Mark `skipped` with "no submission form; curated from HN" unless a Show HN post is planned.
2. If the user posts a Show HN that reaches the front page, check Friday's issue and mark `live` with the issue link.

## Make the listing perform
- Issues group links by theme (#Show HN, #Code, #Design...). A Show HN that gets real discussion is the candidate.
- Post Show HN on a weekday morning US Eastern so it has the full week to be picked up before Friday.

## Gotchas
- Don't email the curator to ask for inclusion; there's no public channel for it.

## After submitting
- Nothing to follow up beyond the HN post itself.
