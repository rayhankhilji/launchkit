# r/thesidehustle

About 243k members talking about side income. Smaller and looser than r/sidehustle. Posting about your own hustle is allowed with the **Support My Hustle** flair, but "stealth advertising", referral links, mentorships and course sales are removed with a 7-day to permanent ban.

## Before you start
- Fits products that someone could run as a side hustle, or tools that help side hustlers. A pure B2B SaaS pitch doesn't fit.
- Write it as a founder update or story ("I built this on nights and weekends, here's where it is"), not an ad.
- Reddit login (Google, Apple, or email/username). The post form needs a login. Flairs below were read from recent posts.

## Field map

## Steps
1. Open https://www.reddit.com/r/thesidehustle/submit, choose **Text**, write the title, pick "Support My Hustle".
2. Write the body story-first, with the link as the last line.
3. `launchkit draft r-thesidehustle -` with title, flair, body, then `launchkit mark r-thesidehustle drafted`.
4. Only if `allow_social_posting` is true and the user approved: post, then `launchkit mark r-thesidehustle submitted --listing-url <url>`.

## Make the listing perform
- Most posts here are "I need ideas" requests with a few upvotes. A real build story with numbers stands out.
- Ask for one kind of feedback (pricing, landing page, first customers).
- Evenings and weekends US time, when the hobby crowd is online.

## Gotchas
- Offers of paid work or tasks need modmail approval first. Posts without it are treated as malicious.
- No DMing users who didn't ask.

## After submitting
- Reply in the thread, not by DM.
