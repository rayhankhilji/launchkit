# Tech in Asia

Subscriber-funded tech news site (YC W15) covering Asia-Pacific startups, deals and big tech, plus a company database and job board. Pitches go by email to the editors. Worth it for startups based in or expanding into Asia-Pacific, ideally with an exclusive.

## Before you start
- Best fit: `company.country` in Asia-Pacific, or a clear APAC expansion story. Otherwise mark `skipped` ("not APAC").
- Their startup guide (https://techinasia.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/17000012882-how-do-i-get-covered-by-tech-in-asia-) says they love exclusives, or news given two weeks before other media.
- Company database: no public "add company" form was visible on https://www.techinasia.com/companies (it loads behind login, unconfirmed). Don't create an account to look for one.

## Field map

| Field on the site | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| To | email | `editors@techinasia.com` |
| Subject | under 80 chars | "Exclusive: " + the headline you'd want them to write |
| Body | under 150 words | Answer their checklist: why now, what is changing, what's the surprise, why should someone outside the country care. Use `copy.press_pitch`, `company.funding_raised`, `company.city` |
| Links | inline | `product.url`, `links.press_kit` |
| Sign-off | text | `founder.name`, `founder.role`, `founder.email` |

## Steps
1. Open https://www.techinasia.com/about and confirm the "Media" contact still says editors@techinasia.com.
2. Read the startup guide linked above. Look at recent coverage in the company's sector.
3. Write the email; give the headline in the subject. Offer exclusivity or a two-week head start if the news isn't public.
4. `lk draft tech-in-asia -` and mark `drafted`. Send only with user approval.

## Make the listing perform
- They write for paying subscribers who work in tech and VC. Funding, M&A, notable hires and market-moving moves get covered; feature launches rarely do.
- Profiles of "up-and-coming tech companies" are a separate format. Pitch the founder story with traction numbers from the profile.
- Founders can also pitch individual reporters, but the guide warns they may miss it; editors@ is the safe default.

## Gotchas
- They don't take payment for coverage. Paid content is Tech in Asia Studios; skip with budget 0.
- Not every exclusive is accepted. Check the fit against existing coverage first.

## After submitting
- No guaranteed reply. If the news is time-bound, give a clear embargo date and don't chase more than once.
