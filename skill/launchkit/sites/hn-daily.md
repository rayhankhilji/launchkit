# HN Daily

Colin Percival's automated daily digest: "the 10 highest-rated articles on Hacker News" each day that haven't appeared before, also as RSS. Nobody picks or accepts submissions. A product gets in only by reaching the top of Hacker News that day, so there is nothing to pitch.

## Before you start
- No form, no email, no account. Don't contact the author.
- The real work is the Show HN post (see the Hacker News guide and `copy.show_hn_title` / `copy.show_hn_body`).

## Field map

| Field | Fill with |
|---|---|
| (none) | No submission. Inclusion is derived from HN rankings. |

## Steps
1. Don't submit anything here.
2. If the user is doing a Show HN, note in the drafts summary that a strong Show HN can land in HN Daily the next day.
3. `launchkit mark hn-daily skipped --note "automatic digest of top HN stories; no submission"`

## Make the listing perform
- You need a top 10 story for that UTC day. Show HN posts reach it often (several per week in recent digests).
- Good Show HN habits apply: a clear title starting "Show HN:", a working demo with no signup wall, and the maker answering comments for the first few hours.
- Post once. Reposting or asking for upvotes breaks HN rules and gets flagged.

## Gotchas
- The page's sponsor line (Triplebyte) is stale; there is no paid option.

## After submitting
- If the Show HN made the front page, check https://www.daemonology.net/hn-daily/ the next day and save the link as social proof.
