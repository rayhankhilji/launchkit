# VentureBeat

Now positioned as "AI news and analysis for enterprise leaders" (beats: orchestration, infrastructure, data, security). Good fit for AI, data and enterprise developer tools; consumer apps are off-topic. Two free routes: a news pitch by email, or a non-promotional guest article for its DataDecisionMakers section.

## Before you start
- Fit check: `product.categories` include AI, data, security or enterprise infrastructure, and `product.target_audience` is technical buyers. Otherwise mark `skipped` ("not a fit").
- News route: needs a time-sensitive hook (funding, launch with numbers, benchmark results).
- Guest route: needs a fully written 800-1,200 word article as an editable Google Doc. This is a writing task for the user; draft it only if asked.

## Field map

News pitch (email):

| Field | Limit / format | Fill with |
|---|---|---|
| To | email | the reporter for the beat (AI: Carl Franzen, Michael Nuñez, Emilia David; data: Shubham Sharma, per the contact page) |
| Cc | email | `tips@venturebeat.com` (the page asks to cc tips@ on time-sensitive news) |
| Subject | under 80 chars | headline with the number |
| Body | under 150 words | `copy.press_pitch` aimed at enterprise tech buyers, embargo time, `product.url`, `links.docs`, `links.press_kit` |

Guest post (Typeform at https://r39crwmcu9m.typeform.com/to/NEzWFTji, fields not viewed): link to an editable Google Doc with commenting allowed, author name, one-sentence bio with the only company link.

## Steps
1. Open https://venturebeat.com/contact-2 and check the reporter list.
2. Draft the email to the reporter, cc tips@. `lk draft venturebeat -`, mark `drafted`. Send only with approval.
3. Guest post: read https://venturebeat.com/guest-posts/, write the article in a Google Doc set to "anyone with the link can comment", then submit the Typeform with user approval.

## Make the listing perform
- Reporters want technical substance: architecture, benchmarks, cost numbers, a customer using it in production.
- For guest posts they look for "technical deep dives" into LLMs, agents and databases. No product pitch; links to the company only in the bio, or the piece is rejected.

## Gotchas
- Sponsored posts (sales@venturebeat.com) are paid. Skip with budget 0.
- Guest docs that are locked or don't allow comments are ignored; acceptance is communicated as a comment on the draft.
- The old /contact/ URL redirects to /contact-2.

## After submitting
- News: no auto-reply. Guest posts: expect a comment on the Google Doc within 14 business days if accepted.
